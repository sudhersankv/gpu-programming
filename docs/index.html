What to conclude

  • No bulk KV H→D restore on a hit — shared KV stays in GPU HBM.
  • Events timeline (below): cache ON shows a burst of tiny Host→Device copies before kernels; cache OFF does not.
  • Client TTFT drops on the second shared-prefix question when APC is on.
  • Session CSV totals look alike because load/init swamp the Q2 delta — that is expected, not a failed A/B.
